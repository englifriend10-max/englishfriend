ABOUT ENGLISH GRAMMAR

Grammar is the framework that makes English work

Grammar is the set of rules that tells us how words fit together to make clear, correct sentences. Once you understand how a sentence is built, speaking and writing become easier, more confident and more precise.

Good grammar helps others understand you, and it helps you understand them. It is the friend every English learner needs.
